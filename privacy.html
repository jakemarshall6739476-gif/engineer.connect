Privacy notice

Last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

Engineer Connect is run by Jake Marshall (the "data controller"). Contact: .

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed); nobody can read them.
  • Your profile: name, job title, engineering area, town or city, skills and what you're looking for. You choose what to fill in.
  • What you post: ideas, ideas you back, connection requests, job adverts and saved job templates.
  • On your device only: your chosen area and a few display settings are kept in your browser's storage and never sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your profile and posts to other members (so we can provide the service you signed up for).
  • To keep the site safe, for example removing spam or abuse (our legitimate interest).

We don't sell your data, show adverts based on it, or use tracking cookies.

Who can see it

  • Your profile and posts can be seen by anyone visiting the site, unless you untick "Show my profile on the Connections page".
  • Connection requests can only be seen by you and the other person.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.

Where it's stored

  • Accounts and posts are stored with Supabase, on servers in London, UK.
  • The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some code is delivered by jsDelivr. Like any website host, they may log your IP address to deliver pages and protect against attacks.
  • Links to news stories and other websites take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account (on the My account page) permanently removes your account, profile and everything you posted.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your information at any time on the My account page, or email us to ask for a copy of it. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
